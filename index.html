About KLU Marketplace

What we are

KLU Marketplace is a student-run classifieds platform for the college community. We connect buyers and sellers on campus for textbooks, gadgets, furniture and more — reducing waste and helping students save money.

  • Local, secure transactions
  • Verified student profiles (coming soon)
  • Low fees — community focused

Why choose us

Built for students — quick listings, clear communication, and campus pickup options make trading easy and safe.

Made by students • For students • Campus-first
